Open Datasets in Learning Analytics: Trends, Challenges, and Best PRACTICE

A systematic survey of 1,125 papers from three flagship learning analytics conferences (LAK, EDM, AIED) between 2020–2024 finds that 172 open datasets exist in the field, 143 of which were missing from all prior surveys — revealing a significant gap in dataset visibility and reproducibility infrastructure. The findings surface concrete best-practice recommendations for dataset sharing in AI-in-education research.
