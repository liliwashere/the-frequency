More Articles Are Now Created by AI Than Humans

Original empirical research sampling 43,000 URLs from CommonCrawl found AI-generated articles surpassed human-written content by November 2024, with growth plateauing since May 2024 as AI content underperforms in search rankings. Detection was validated at a 0.6% false negative rate on GPT-4o-generated articles — this is the kind of rigorous baseline measurement the AI content debate has been missing.

To Understand AI, Think Like A Dragonfly

Georgetown/ANU international law professor Anthea Roberts maps nine distinct AI stakeholder perspectives — from builders and displaced workers to geopolitical hawks and environmentalists — to argue that single-lens AI governance is structurally blind. The framework is practical: each lens catches risks the others miss, and policy that ignores any one of them will fail on its own terms.

Research

Using AI for Just 10 Minutes Might Make You Lazy and Dumb, Study Shows - WIRED

A multi-institution study (CMU, MIT, Oxford, UCLA) found that just 10 minutes of AI-assisted problem-solving measurably reduced performance when the tool was removed — participants were more likely to give up or fail without it. Lead researcher Michiel Bakker frames the finding not as a ban argument but as a design challenge: models should sometimes prioritize a user's learning over giving them the answer.

After Anthropic refused to release Claude Mythos over cybersecurity risks, the Trump administration reversed course and signed voluntary safety-testing agreements with Google DeepMind, Microsoft, and xAI — rebranding the US AI Safety Institute but keeping its function. The piece documents how a single withheld model release reshaped federal AI policy in weeks.
