Trump loses more control over AI regulation as Illinois passes landmark law - Ars Technica

Illinois passed the strongest state-level AI safety law in the US, requiring frontier AI labs to publish safety plans, submit to independent third-party testing, and report critical incidents within 24–72 hours — days after the Trump administration dropped its own federal oversight plan.
