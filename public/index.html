Fine-Tuning vs Context Engineering: A 2026 decision framework
Fine-tuning versus context engineering is a real budget decision, and guessing wrong costs either weeks of GPU time or a brittle prompt that breaks on the next model update. karrierewelt.golem.de
ChatGPT verändert das Gehirn: Neue Studie offenbart kognitive Kosten der KI-Nutzung
MIT Media Lab researchers found that regular ChatGPT use for writing tasks produces what they term 'cognitive debt' — measurable reduction in independent cognitive performance when AI assistance is removed. The study compared ChatGPT users against control groups writing unaided, tracking both neural activity and output quality over time.
Originally in GermanResearchers at MIT Media Lab have examined how regular ChatGPT use for writing affects the brain. Long-term use can lead to an 'accumulation of cognitive debt' — reduced cognitive performance when working without AI assistance. The study specifically examined neural and cognitive effects in a group using ChatGPT to write essays, compared to a control group writing unaided.

Le Monde · lemonde.fr
L’essor de la recherche avec une IA, sur ChatGPT ou Google, fait craindre aux médias une perte de trafic
Chartbeat data across 2,500 news sites shows Google search-referred traffic to media dropped 33% in a single year (November 2024 to November 2025), as AI-generated answers increasingly substitute for clicking through. Le Monde reports that the Reuters Institute's annual study has crystallised fears in French and European newsrooms about structural traffic collapse.
Originally in FrenchOn January 12, a series of figures published in the Reuters Institute's annual report crystallised fears across the media sector: traffic to news sites from Google searches fell by 33% in the space of a single year, between November 2024 and November 2025, according to data collected by analytics firm Chartbeat across a sample of 2,500 media sites worldwide. The shift toward AI-powered search on ChatGPT and Google is accelerating this structural change, with newsrooms increasingly alarmed about a new dependency they cannot control.

Fran de Olza · frandeolza.substack.com
La Carrera Hacia la AGI: Quién Controla el Dinero de la Inteligencia ...
Between 2025 and 2026, the leading AI labs secured over $70 billion in cumulative contracts for AI workloads — and the piece maps who controls the capital flows behind the AGI race. The analysis focuses on the structural question of whether compute ownership determines who gets to define what AGI means and when it arrives.
Originally in SpanishIn 2025–2026, the major AI companies secured more than $70 billion in cumulative contracts for artificial intelligence workloads. The race toward AGI is not only a technical competition — it is a contest over who controls the money, the compute, and ultimately the definition of what it means to reach artificial general intelligence. Whoever controls the capital controls the narrative.

No Priors · Elad Gil & Sarah Guo
The most technically honest AI investor podcast
