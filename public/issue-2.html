Google’s SynthID AI watermarking tech is being adopted by OpenAI, Nvidia, and more - Ars Technica

Google's SynthID watermarking standard — embedded invisibly in pixels, video frames, and audio waveforms — is now being adopted by OpenAI and Nvidia, alongside the C2PA metadata standard. The piece also notes the core limitation: SynthID only marks content from models that implement it, leaving the majority of AI images on the internet uncovered.
