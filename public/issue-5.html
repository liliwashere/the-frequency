Rest of World · restofworld.org
Scarcity is driving AI innovation outside Silicon Valley - Rest of World
Rest of World reports that compute scarcity in India, Brazil, the UAE, and Africa is producing distinct AI infrastructure stacks — designed around constraint rather than abundance — with builders like Dizzaract in Abu Dhabi leading the charge. arstechnica.com
Mathematicians warn of AI threats to profession as industry encroaches - Ars Technica
The International Mathematical Union-endorsed Leiden Declaration warns that AI-generated mathematical drafts are 'cheap to produce' but risk cluttering the literature with wrong results, and that tech industry logic is fundamentally incompatible with mathematical values. The declaration, co-authored by Columbia's Michael Harris, is a formal professional response to AI's encroachment on a domain that underpins the field itself.
Research Read →
multipolarite.substack.com
Géopolitique de l'intelligence artificielle
Cette analyse examine la géopolitique de l'IA à travers les investissements massifs en infrastructure — Microsoft s'engage à dépenser 80 milliards de dollars en centres de données IA pour son seul exercice fiscal 2025 — et l'émergence de DeepSeek comme signal que la Chine peut désormais concurrencer les laboratoires américains à une fraction du coût. L'auteur argumente que la compétition en IA remodèle les flux de capital à une échelle historique.
Originally in FrenchAt the heart of this competition lies a spectacular surge in AI infrastructure investment — advanced microprocessors and hyperscale data centers, often called AI 'gigafactories.' Microsoft alone plans to spend around $80 billion in its fiscal year 2025 building new AI-capable data centers — a significant jump from the $53 billion spent in 2023. China has intensified support for domestic players, from chipmakers to cloud providers, and for emerging players like DeepSeek, which surprised the world in early 2025 by releasing a state-of-the-art AI model at a fraction of the cost estimated by leading US labs.

techcabal.com
How Shell Foundation and 500 Global structure risk in African climate tech
TechCabal reports how Shell Foundation and 500 Global built a structured risk-sharing accelerator specifically for African climate tech startups, with Juliette Keeley and Carrie Liauw explaining how global mentors and capital are being channelled toward founders in markets that will define the world's carbon footprint. The piece goes beyond the announcement to detail mechanics: what the fund actually pays for and how exits are planned.

thegradient.pub
Machine Learning Won't Solve Natural Language Understanding
The Gradient publishes a pointed argument that data-driven ML methods are suited to language *processing* tasks but are structurally irrelevant to language *understanding*, tracing the intellectual history of NLP back to Kenneth Church's pragmatic empiricism. The piece makes a clear, falsifiable claim that most of the field prefers to avoid.

nidly.substack.com
Don’t Waste 2026 on the Wrong Career: ML vs AI Engineer
A crisp comparison of ML Engineering vs AI Engineering drawn from production realities: ML engineers own model evaluation and data-shift degradation; AI engineers own prompt strategies, caching, fallback paths, and inference observability. The framing cuts through title inflation and gives practitioners a decision framework based on what the work actually involves.

Shaping the Future of AI Policy: A Q&A with Shamira Ahmed - Partnership on AI
Shamira Ahmed — founder of DepHUB, the first independent AI think tank founded by an Indigenous African woman, based in South Africa — argues that the UN must centre diversity and equity in AI policy rather than defaulting to Western technology frameworks. She identifies specific structural barriers to AI adoption in the Global South and ties them to the Global Digital Compact.

No Priors · Elad Gil & Sarah Guo
The most technically honest AI investor podcast
